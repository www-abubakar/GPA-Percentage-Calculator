The formulas, in plain terms

Approximate GPA

GPA = Average Percentage ÷ 9.5

Average all your subjects' percentages, then divide by 9.5. Example: an average of 82.6% gives an approximate GPA of 8.69.

Percentage

Percentage = (Marks Obtained ÷ Total Marks) × 100

Example: 413 ÷ 500 × 100 = 82.6%

Good to know

Frequently asked questions

What is GPA?

GPA stands for Grade Point Average. It's a single number that summarizes your academic performance by converting each grade into a point value and averaging those points, typically weighted by credit hours.

How is GPA calculated?

This calculator averages the percentage you enter for each subject, then divides that average by 9.5: Approximate GPA = Average Percentage ÷ 9.5. Some institutions instead calculate official GPA from letter grades and credit hours, so always confirm the exact method your school uses.

How do I convert percentage to GPA?

A widely used approximation is GPA = Percentage ÷ 9.5. For example, 82.6% gives an approximate GPA of 8.69. This tool labels that result "Approximate GPA" for that reason.

Is percentage-to-GPA conversion exact?

No — it's an approximation, not an official conversion. Different boards, schools, and countries use different scales and formulas, so always confirm the exact method used by your own institution for anything official.

How do I calculate percentage from marks?

Percentage = (Marks Obtained ÷ Total Marks) × 100. For example, scoring 413 out of 500 marks gives 82.6%.

Does every school or college use the same GPA scale?

No. Common scales include 4.0, 5.0, and 10.0, each with its own grade-point mapping, and some institutions define their own entirely. Always check your school's official grading policy when it matters for your records.
